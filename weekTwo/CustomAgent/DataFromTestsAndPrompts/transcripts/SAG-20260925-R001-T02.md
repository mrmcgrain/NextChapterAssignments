Soft & Gooey Cinnamon Rolls

Makes: 12 rolls
Prep: 25 minutes + 1½–2 hours rising
Bake: 22–27 minutes

Ingredients

Dough
- ¾ cup (180 ml) whole milk, warm
- 2¼ teaspoons active dry or instant yeast
- ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- ¼ cup (57 g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3½ cups (420 g) all-purpose flour, plus more if needed

Cinnamon filling
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- ¾ cup (150 g) packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

Cream cheese frosting
- 4 ounces (113 g) cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup (57 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (120 g) powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1–2 tablespoons milk

Start the dough. Warm the milk until it feels comfortably warm, not hot—about 105–110°F (40–43°C). Stir in the yeast and a teaspoon of the sugar. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes, until slightly foamy.

Mix. In a large bowl, combine the yeast mixture, remaining sugar, eggs, melted butter, and salt. Gradually mix in the flour until a soft dough forms.

Knead. Knead by hand for about 8–10 minutes, or with a stand mixer and dough hook for 5–7 minutes. The finished dough should be smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. Avoid adding too much extra flour.

First rise. Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm place for about 60–90 minutes, or until roughly doubled in size.

Make the filling. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon, and pinch of salt in a small bowl.

Roll out the dough. Turn the risen dough onto a lightly floured surface. Roll it into approximately a 14 × 18-inch rectangle.

Fill and roll. Spread the softened butter evenly over the dough, leaving about ½ inch uncovered along one long edge. Sprinkle the cinnamon-sugar mixture evenly over the butter. Starting from the opposite long edge, roll the dough tightly into a log. Pinch the seam closed.

Cut the rolls. Slice the log into 12 equal pieces. Dental floss or a very sharp knife works well without crushing the rolls.

Second rise. Arrange the rolls in a greased 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Cover and let rise for another 30–45 minutes, until noticeably puffy.

Bake. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake for approximately 22–27 minutes, until the tops are lightly golden and the centers are cooked through. Don't overbake if you want soft centers.

Make the frosting. Beat the cream cheese and butter until smooth. Beat in the powdered sugar and vanilla. Add milk a little at a time until the frosting reaches your preferred consistency.

Finish. Let the cinnamon rolls cool for about 10 minutes, then spread the frosting over them while they're still warm.

For especially gooey rolls, frost them while warm enough that some of the frosting melts down between the layers.
